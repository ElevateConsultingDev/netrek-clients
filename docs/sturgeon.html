STURGEON

Ship upgrades & special weapons  ·  what this server runs

In ordinary Netrek every ship of a given type is identical. STURGEON changes that: you spend your kills to buy permanent upgrades and special weapons that make your ship stronger and stick with you from life to life. Rack up kills, refit, and turn credit into shields, faster torps, plasmas, nukes — and keep flying a ship you built.

The idea in one paragraph

Kills are currency. Each kill adds to your upgrade credit. At a repair/home planet you refit to open the upgrade menu and spend that credit on things like stronger shields, a hotter phaser, more fuel, cloak, or an arsenal of special weapons. Upgrades persist across deaths — up to a cap tied to your rank. The better you play, the more monster your ship becomes.

The currency: kills

  • Every kill you earn accumulates upgrade credit.
  • Each upgrade has a price measured in kills — a base cost plus more for every level you've already bought of it (so the 3rd shield boost costs more than the 1st).
  • There's a rank cap: the total you can hold in upgrades is limited by your rank. Rank up and you can carry more. Spend beyond your rank and, when you die, you'll lose upgrades back down to what your rank supports (winning by genocide/conquer lets you keep everything).
  • Starbases carry no upgrades — refitting to a base clears them.

Exact prices are shown in-game: open the upgrade menu → Misc → current status to see every upgrade you own and the kill cost of the next one.

How to upgrade

  1. Get near a repair or your home planet (where you can refit).
  2. Start a refit. The client shows the upgrade menu as messages (lines beginning UPG->).
  3. Navigate with the number keys 0–9 — while you're in the upgrade menu, the number keys pick menu options (0 always aborts/backs out).
  4. Drill into a category, pick an item, and your credit is spent. Repeat until you're out of credit or done.

On the Netrek Client of Mac, make sure your message window is visible so you can read the whole menu (it's a two-line prompt).

The upgrade menu

Top level: 1=normal refit, 2=shields/hull, 3=engines, 4=weapons, 5=special weapons, 6=one time, 7=misc (0 aborts). Each opens a sub-menu:

  1. 2 — Shields / Hull
    1 permanent shield  ·  2 temporary shield  ·  3 permanent hull.
    Tougher to kill: more shield capacity and hull.
  2. 3 — Engines
    1 fuel  ·  2 recharge  ·  3 max warp  ·  4 acceleration  ·  5 deceleration  ·  6 cooling.
    Go faster, turn/stop quicker, hold more fuel, and run cooler.
  3. 4 — Weapons
    1 phaser damage  ·  2 torp speed  ·  3 torp fuse (range)  ·  4 weapon cooling.
    Hit harder and reach further before your weapons overheat.
  4. 5 — Special weapons — buy the arsenal below. (5 = inventory, to see what you're carrying.)
  5. 6 — One time
    1 fire while cloaked  ·  2 detonate your own torps for damage.
    Permanent trick unlocks — bought once.
  6. 7 — Misc
    1 cloak  ·  2 tractor strength  ·  3 tractor range  ·  4 repair rate  ·  5 current status  ·  6 undo next upgrade.
    current status lists what you own and next costs; undo refunds the last upgrade.

Special weapons

Beyond torps and phasers, STURGEON lets you buy limited-ammo heavy weapons. Select/toggle your active special weapon with the coup key (Shift+C) and fire it. Each costs fuel to launch.

WeaponVariantsWhat it is
PlasmasType 1–5, plus pseudoplasma Big, slow, high-damage guided torpedoes. Higher types hit harder. Pseudoplasma is a decoy.
Nukes10 / 20 / 50 / 100 megaton Warheads with a large blast radius — small, medium, big, huge. Area devastation.
Suicide drones— Launch-and-forget attack drones (high fuel cost).
Mines— Drop persistent mines that damage whoever runs into them.

Special weapons are ammo-limited — you buy a quantity in the upgrade menu (5 = special weapons) and check what's left with inventory. The server god can disable special weapons; if so you'll be told when you try.

Dying, ranks & keeping your ship

  • Upgrades you can afford within your rank stay with you when you die — you respawn already kitted out.
  • If you've over-extended (more upgrades than your rank credit), dying strips upgrades until you're back under the cap. So rank matters: it's both your budget and your insurance.
  • Winning the galaxy (genocide or conquer) lets you keep everything, over-cap or not.

On this server

The Elevate server runs STURGEON + NEWBIE — the full upgrade system, with robots filling out the game so there's always someone to kill (and to fund your upgrades). Connect, rack up kills, refit, and build your ship.
